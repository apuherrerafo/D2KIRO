## 8. Limitations of this evidence

- Bans are simulated by the Simulator's own policy from `hero_patch_stats` pick volume (the project has no real ban-rate data); the Enemy Bot and the three Player policies are scripted. Absolute rates describe this simulator, not live play.
- One meta snapshot, mono-patch, sync-stale (`metaIsStale` is forced true here, as in the Wave 4A audit); `hero-counters.json` / `hero-positions.json` are curated, not patch-verified (WAVE4_DATA_READINESS.md).
- Drafts within a seed set share the same snapshot and are strongly correlated; counts are descriptive, no confidence interval is claimed.
- Quality of the recommendations (is the advice *good Dota*?) is NOT judged here — that is the independent Dota Judge's job (`WAVE5_DOTA_JUDGE.*`).
